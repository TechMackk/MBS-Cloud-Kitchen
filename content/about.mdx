# Our Story

MBS Cloud Kitchen was born from a simple belief: food should nourish the mind, body, and soul.

Founded in Hyderabad, we specialize in authentic Telangana cuisine — recipes passed down through generations, prepared with the same care your grandmother would.

## Why "Cloud Kitchen"?

We focus 100% on the food, not the dining experience. No fancy interiors. No bloated menus. Just real, honest Telangana cooking delivered fresh to your door.

## Our Commitment

- **No frozen ingredients.** Ever.
- **No reused oil.** Every batch fresh.
- **No artificial colors or preservatives.**
- **Daily kitchen audits** for hygiene and quality.
