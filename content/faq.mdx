# Frequently Asked Questions

## What are your hours of operation?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and Sunday from 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Orders placed near closing time are fulfilled based on kitchen capacity.

## Which areas do you deliver to?

We currently deliver across Hyderabad, with a focus on central and western zones including Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Gachibowli, Madhapur, and surrounding neighbourhoods. For locations outside our standard zone, please WhatsApp us to confirm.

## Is there a minimum order value?

There is no strict minimum for regular orders, but very small orders may have a nominal delivery charge depending on distance. Catering orders require a minimum of 10 guests.

## How do you handle allergens?

We prepare veg, egg, and non-veg dishes in a hygienic kitchen with careful separation practices. Please mention any allergies or dietary restrictions in your order notes or WhatsApp message so we can advise you accurately.

## How do I place an order?

Browse our menu at mbscloudkitchen.in/menu, add items to your cart, and complete checkout. You can also order directly via WhatsApp at +91 81796 56696 with your dish choices and delivery address.

## How far in advance should I book catering?

We recommend booking catering at least 7–10 days in advance for events under 100 guests. Larger events (100+ guests) should be booked 2–3 weeks ahead. Last-minute requests are accepted subject to availability.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery, UPI, and bank transfer for catering bookings. Online payment gateway integration is coming soon.

## What is your cancellation policy?

Regular orders can be cancelled before preparation begins — WhatsApp us immediately. Catering cancellations made 48+ hours before the event receive a full refund of any advance paid. Cancellations within 48 hours may incur a partial charge.

## Do you separate veg and non-veg cooking?

Yes. We follow strict kitchen hygiene with separate utensils and preparation areas for vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes. Our veg dishes contain no meat, fish, or egg unless marked as egg.

## What cooking oil do you use?

We use fresh oil for every batch — we never reuse frying oil. Sunflower and rice bran oil are used for most dishes; traditional recipes may use ghee where appropriate.

## Can I customize spice levels?

Absolutely. Most dishes can be prepared mild, medium, or extra spicy. Mention your preference in order notes or tell us on WhatsApp. Some traditional Telangana dishes are naturally spicy — we'll always warn you.

## Do you offer bulk order discounts?

Orders above ₹5,000 may qualify for a discount — contact us on WhatsApp to discuss. Catering packages are priced per plate and scale with guest count and menu selection.

## What food safety certifications do you have?

Our kitchen follows FSSAI hygiene guidelines with daily cleaning audits, temperature-controlled storage, and staff trained in food safety. We are in the process of formal FSSAI registration display — ask us for current status.

## What is your refund policy?

If there is a quality issue with your order, contact us within 2 hours with a photo. We will replace the item or issue a refund at our discretion. Genuine complaints are always resolved fairly.
