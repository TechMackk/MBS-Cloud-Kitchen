# MBS Cloud Kitchen Policies

## Quality Promise

MBS Cloud Kitchen stands behind four non-negotiable promises: no frozen ingredients, no reused oil, fresh daily preparation, and a hygienic kitchen audited every day. Every dish leaving our kitchen is made to order — never reheated from bulk batches.

## Hygiene Standards

Our kitchen team follows a strict hygiene protocol: hand sanitization before every prep session, separate cutting boards for veg and non-veg, daily deep cleaning of all surfaces, and temperature logs for refrigerated storage. Delivery packaging is sealed and labelled with preparation time.

## Brand Story

MBS — Mind, Body & Soul — was founded in Hyderabad to bring authentic Telangana flavours to your doorstep. We believe food should nourish all three. Our recipes come from generations of home cooks in Telangana, prepared with the same care your family would. We are a cloud kitchen focused entirely on quality food, not dining ambience.
